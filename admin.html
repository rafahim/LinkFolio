Static hosting cannot write files from your browser. Follow these steps to publish.

1. Export config.json from this page.Download it from the export control above.
2. Replace your project’s config.json.Keep the filename exactly config.json.
3. Deploy the updated project.Commit to GitHub Pages or redeploy to your static host.
